Study Goals

My Personal SWOT as a Student

Strengths

  1. Interests: I enjoy learning more about programming and how computers work.
  2. Analytical thinking: I can break problems down into small steps.

Weaknesses

  1. Communication: I find it stressful to speak with others or give presentations.
  2. Asking for help: I try to figure things out alone for too long before asking.
  3. Poor focus: It takes some time before I get into the flow of what I'm doing, and I'm easily distracted.

Opportunities

  1. Learning: more about how computers work and getting a HBO-ICT diploma.
  2. Employment: in the ICT sector, likely working as a software developer.
  3. Online courses and working on personal projects alongside school.

Threats

  1. Public Transport: If my bus/boat/train gets delayed or canceled, I won't be able to arrive on time.
  2. Loud Environments: They distract my focus and can trigger my tinnitus which can give me a headache.

SMART Study Goals

Goal 1
(Strength)

I'm going to use my interest in programming and computers to create a C++ Tetris clone.

Specific/Measurable: I will keep a log of every time I work on this project, I will work on it at least 2 hours a week and I will not give up until it's completed.

Time-bound: I will have a working demo by Christmas 2026.

Goal 2
(Weakness)

I'm going to address my poor focus by reducing distractions and making a clear overview of everything that needs to be done.

Specific/Measurable: Before I start working on a project or an assignment, I will make a clear overview of everything that needs to be done and simplify larger tasks into smaller and easier tasks. I will work in a quiet environment and use noice cancelling headphones with calming background music to reduce distractions.

Time-bound: I will evaluate this goal after completing the first goal (Christmas 2026).

My Mission this Year

As a HBO-ICT student, I am working toward becoming a professional software developer. My interests in computers and programming means I enjoy learning more about this field. This year I am learning to improve my focus, so I'm less easily distracted and always have a clear overview of everything that needs to be done. My goal is to have a working C++ Tetris clone by Christmas 2026 as proof of my growth.
