About me

My name is Maurits, I'm 20 years old and I follow the HBO-ICT programme at HZ University of Applied Sciences. I like playing violin and collecting physical media such as movies, music and games. I have a collection of over 200 movies, they're all backed up on an old laptop that I use as a home server, this way I can watch them anywhere I go. I'm good at following instructions like building lego sets and working on clearly explained assignments. My reason for choosing the HBO-ICT programme is that I want to become a better programmer, my goal is to become good enough to be able to build my own apps and games on different platforms. Even though I like making things, I find it difficult to create something original.
